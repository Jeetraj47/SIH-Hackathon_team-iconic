🧪 Physics-Informed Arrhenius Spoilage Equation

The mathematical formulation powering real-time agricultural cargo shelf-life prediction in reefer containers:

k(T, H, v) = A · exp(-Ea / (R · T)) · (1 + γ · H/100) · (1 + β · v)
• k(T, H, v): Spoilage kinetic rate multiplier
• A: Product-specific pre-exponential factor (e.g. Lakadong Turmeric)
• Ea: Bio-chemical activation energy (kJ/mol)
• R: Universal gas constant (8.314 J/mol·K)
• T: Sensor telemetry container temperature (Kelvin)
• H: Relative humidity percentage modifier (γ = 0.35)
• v: Road vibration frequency spectrum modifier (β = 0.12)
💡 Operational Advantage:

Enables dynamic rerouting *before* produce quality degrades below government public distribution standards, saving up to ₹42 Lakhs per transit batch.
